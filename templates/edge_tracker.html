How The Edge picks — the model

A market-data-only momentum model (no fundamentals). The core signal is acceleration — whether a stock's momentum is speeding up or slowing down:

accel = ( Pt / Pt−3mo − 1 )  − ( Pt−3mo / Pt−6mo − 1 )

i.e. the most recent 3-month return minus the prior 3-month return. Positive = momentum building.

Each rebalance (~2-month clock):

  1. Universe — top ~1,000 US names by market cap (Russell-1000 proxy).
  2. Liquidity floor — keep only names ≥ $2B market cap (tradeable size).
  3. Rank by the acceleration signal, highest first.
  4. Correlation cap — admit a name only if it's ≤ 0.50 correlated with every holding already in the basket (so it never becomes eight versions of one bet).
  5. Hold the top N equal-weight (selector above; product = 10 stocks, the 7-stock research book is paper-tracked alongside).
  6. Regime switch — if the S&P 500 is below its 200-day average, cut to 25% invested (rest in cash) to dodge crashes.
  7. Costs — returns are shown net of ~10bps trading costs.
  8. Growth mix — optionally draw part of the basket (default 75%) from names with ≥15% YoY revenue growth; the selector above adjusts it (Off–100%).

Honest caveats: history is survivorship-inflated (missing delisted names), short-window returns are lumpy and tail-driven, and the recent era has been unusually momentum-friendly — trust the risk-adjusted shape (Sharpe, drawdown) over the headline CAGR.
